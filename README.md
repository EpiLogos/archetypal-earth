# The Archetypal Field

*An interlinked wiki of C.G. Jung's archetypes, traced as he traced them — every claim cited to volume, paragraph, and page.*

---

## What this is

This is a map of Jung's archetypal field: every archetype he distinguished, every image-family through which it appears, and every concrete instance of those images he discussed — a 1900 BCE Egyptian pyramid text, a 4th-century gnostic diagram, a 16th-century alchemical woodcut, a dream told at the Zürich seminar table in 1930, a physicist's nightmare in 1955 — all in one navigable structure, openable in [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md).

Jung's own method was **amplification**: to understand a symbol, you line up its parallels across cultures and centuries until the underlying pattern shows itself. Nobody can hold that whole field in their head — and the printed *Collected Works* scatters it across twenty volumes and forty years of revision. This vault reassembles it. Pick any symbol — the serpent, the mandala, the philosopher's stone — and read its trajectory through time, space, and Jung's own evolving thought, with every stop on the journey cited back to the exact paragraph where he wrote it.

## The corpus

The foundation is Jung's complete published psychology, machine-extracted from 28 volumes and verified page by page:

- **The Collected Works**, vols. 1–18 and both halves of vol. 9 — from the 1900s psychiatric experiments through the alchemical summations (*Psychology and Alchemy*, *Mysterium Coniunctionis*) to *The Undiscovered Self*
- **All six seminars** — Dream Analysis, Visions (the great 1,500-page patient series), Nietzsche's Zarathustra, Children's Dreams, Analytical Psychology 1925, Kundalini Yoga
- **The Pauli correspondence** (*Atom and Archetype*)
- The Zofingia student lectures and the standalone *Synchronicity* monograph

**16,077 pages · ~19,200 harvested records · every paragraph of Jung's own numbering indexed** (12,500+ entries) and machine-checkable.

## Why it was built this way

Three design decisions shape everything here, and each one guards a specific scholarly value.

**1. The three-tier ontology — archetype as such → image → instance.** Jung insisted the archetype *as such* is irrepresentable — a structural pattern known only through the images that express it, which alone have history and geography. The vault encodes exactly that distinction. The irreducible **self**, **shadow**, **anima**, **Great Mother** sit at the top tier, defined only by what Jung actually wrote. Beneath them, representable **image families** (mandala, serpent, ouroboros, conjunctio — 320 of them). Beneath those, the **instances** — 2,700 individual dated, placed appearances: artifacts, myths, rituals, dreams. The rule that a note may only *instance* an image, never an archetype-as-such, is Jung's own epistemology turned into schema.

**2. The citation spine.** Every claim in every note resolves to a volume, paragraph, and page — and a script verifies cited paragraphs against the extracted corpus text. Quotes in the notes were sampled and matched 100% against the source. Where OCR damaged a paragraph marker, reconstructions are flagged and tracked separately, never silently substituted. What you read here can be checked against Jung himself in seconds.

**3. Contradictions are preserved, not smoothed.** Jung revised constantly — the 1912 *Symbols of Transformation* is a different book from its 1952 edition, the concept of *libido* changes meaning between strata, the synchronicity appendix keeps pre-correction probabilities the main text revised away. Where his thought splits, this vault records **both strata with their dates**, side by side. The tensions are the biography of the thought.

Alongside the structure sits the interpretation layer: family deep-dives written from the harvest — the serpent chain from Gnostic Agathodaimon through Kundalini to *Mercurius* to the Self; the 3→4 problem traced from 1900 to 1958; the anima's four-stage ladder with Jung's own counter-scale for the animus; the god-image doctrine in its five-stage evolution. In every cross-cultural table, claims from Jung's text are marked **J** and claims from standard scholarship are marked **S** — nothing smuggled.

## How to use it

Open the folder as an Obsidian vault (install the **Dataview** community plugin — the aggregation views run on it). Start at **INDEX** or **maps/Home**. The graph view renders the whole field; every image note carries a live table of its instances sorted by date; every instance links back through its image family to the archetype it expresses and forward to its parallels.

The `corpus/` folder holds the full normalized text of all 28 volumes — the notes cite into it, and it remains the court of appeal. `LEDGER.md` documents every build decision, every known limitation, and every unresolved question the project has about itself.

## Provenance & scope

Built from the collector's own lawfully owned copies of the Princeton/Bollingen editions and Routledge reprints. It is a personal research instrument — a way of reading Jung that the book format does not allow — not a substitute for the works themselves, and not for redistribution. Where the corpus has gaps (vol. 18's later apparatus, the unpublished Red Book material Jung's Philemon actually comes from), the vault says so in its ledger rather than papering over them.
