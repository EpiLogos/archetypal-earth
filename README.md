# An Archetypal Earth

I built this because nobody can hold Jung's whole field in their head — the Collected Works scatter it across thirty volumes and forty years of revision. This site reassembles it. Pick a symbol — the serpent, the mandala, the philosopher's stone — and read its path through time and cultures, cited to the exact paragraph where Jung wrote it.

Jung's archetypes across place and time — the complete field, navigable. Every archetype he distinguished, every image through which it appears, every dated instance he discussed: from a 1900 BCE pyramid text to a physicist's dream in 1955.

30 volumes of the Collected Works and every major seminar, every claim traceable to volume, paragraph, and page. Built on his own method: amplification — line up the parallels until the pattern shows itself.

Alongside the globe, the project carries doctrine maps written from the harvest: the serpent chain from Gnostic Agathodaimon to the Self; the 3→4 problem traced 1900→1958; the anima's four-stage ladder; the god-image doctrine; the aeon thesis — the fish age ending, Aquarius beginning, and Jung's fifty years of forecasts. In every cross-cultural table, claims from Jung's text are marked J; claims from standard scholarship are marked S. Nothing smuggled.

A personal research edition. Corpus citations from the Princeton/Bollingen and Routledge editions are included for study; the works themselves remain the court of appeal. Portrait: public domain, via Wikimedia Commons. Globe imagery: NASA.
