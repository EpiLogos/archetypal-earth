An Archetypal Earth

Jung's archetypes across place and time — the complete field, navigable. Every archetype he distinguished, every image through which it appears, every dated instance he discussed: from a 1900 BCE pyramid text to a physicist's dream in 1955.

30 volumes of the Collected Works and every major seminar, every claim traceable to volume, paragraph, and page. Built on his own method: amplification — line up the parallels until the pattern shows itself.
