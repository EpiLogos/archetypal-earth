# Burt, Archetypes of the Zodiac — extraction report

Source: Kathleen Burt, *Archetypes of the Zodiac*, Llewellyn Publications.
Copyright page prints **1988** (first edition; LC card 87-45743, ISBN 0-87542-088-5);
the digitized copy in the owner's library is the **1999 ninth printing** (title page
prints 1999). The `year` in `burt.json` is 1988, the printed publication year.

Quotes live in `curation/sky/burt.json`. `subject` names the body whose card
the quote serves; `about` says what the quote characterizes — `planet` (the
body itself), `sign:<Sign>` (the sign, filed under the body that rules or
exalts that chapter), or `ruler-of:<Sign>` (the body in its rulership role).
`pdfPage` is the PDF page index;
`bookPage` is the printed page; **pdf = book + 22** throughout (verified at the
Chapter One opening: pdf 23 = book p. 1). One quote (the Moon's, from the
Introduction) sits on roman-numbered pages, so its `bookPage` is null and the
chapter field says "Introduction".

## How the book is organized

Twelve chapters, one per sign, ordered around the zodiac. Each chapter opens
with the sign's definitional passage (the "Search for …" thesis), then treats the
**mundane ruler**, then the **esoteric ruler** (Burt uses the Alice Bailey
rulership scheme, table at book p. xvii / pdf 19), then myths and case studies,
and closes with a self-test questionnaire. The Introduction (pdf 15–20) defines
archetype, myth, mundane/esoteric rulers and exaltation, and prints the
rulership table.

## Per-body summaries (in Burt's words, with quote references)

**Sun** (pdf 175, 176, 178, 24). The Sun is the solar principle itself: "In Leo,
we arrive at the solar, or Masculine principle—the confident energy of the Sun";
its archetypal signature is constancy, "derived from the orbit of Sol himself"
— a body "always the same orange disc" that "didn't change its shape or go
through phases." Esoterically "the sun represents illumination," and in ruling
Leo it operates "as mundane and as esoteric ruler" at once — the only planet she
gives the same rulership twice. In Aries it is exalted: "An exuberant planet in
an exuberant sign leads to an exalted personality."

**Moon** (pdf 137, 136, 18). "The Moon represents feelings and emotions. She is
an instinctual, intuitive, nurturing planet, but also a planet that fosters
emotional attachment to the form she provides." Cancer's ruler has been called
"the Giver of Form" (Plato included), "a bestower of the gifts of feeling, of
intuition, and of nurturing instincts"; it is "the most feminine planet" ruling
"the most feminine sign," its many phases matching "the many moods of Cancer."

**Mercury** (pdf 96, 95, 211, 212, 30). Mercury is the quicksilver mind: "in
alchemy the 'astrological Mercury' was the fluid, mobile, quicksilver mind. The
mind by itself … is dual and amoral." Hermes is the one god "always in transit
among the three worlds," Guide and Messenger of Olympus, Earth and Hades. As
ruler of earthy Virgo he becomes "less flighty, more grounded, stable and
consistent," the alchemist whose virtues are "patience, purification or
perfectionism, meticulous attention to technique and procedure, obedience to
methodology, industriousness, and most important, humility" — as esoteric ruler
of Aries he is Logos, the disciplined intellect that controls "restless, angry
Mars."

**Venus** (pdf 59, 122, 253, 88, 123). Venus is "the principle of attraction"
with "a strong physical magnetism," Aphrodite, "Goddess of Love, aesthetics and
civilized good taste" — a goddess who "could actually possess mortals … with
her spell of longing, yearning, and love." Esoterically (via Bailey) "she is
harmony—the Eros' power to reconcile opposites … She is the unity principle that
spans the Gates of Hercules"; her positive instincts are "warmth, grace of
self-expression, social comfort, artistic expression, and charm."

**Mars** (pdf 27, 28, 296, 295). Mundane Mars is pure drive: "Mars is
passionate, but not exactly tender or aware of others' feelings!", he "rules
adrenalin" and seeks "to go where no man has gone before"; his lesson is that
"Mars needs to temper Logos with Eros." In Scorpio he turns inward: "not the
outspoken, direct planetary ruler we encountered in Aries … but a much subtler
energy," the retrograde, "sublimated instincts consciously focused upon a
specific goal" — Alice Bailey's esoteric ruler of Scorpio.

**Jupiter** (pdf 349, 350, 369, 455). "Jupiter is the great benefic … it shines
with a bright light"; Zeus the All Highest is so grandiose "that no other planet
could be exalted in his sign," and his Greek key words are "magnanimity, majesty
or power, beneficence, abundance, spirituality, gravity, joviality or optimism,
justice, truth, wisdom." From *Theos* comes "enthusiasm," and "in every
Sagittarian client … there is this pure spark of Zeus' enthusiasm." Esoterically
he is Guru, experienced "through dreams and meditation as inner space, as what
Jung called psychic space," and "Jupiter (opportunity) is the esoteric ruler of
Aquarius."

**Saturn** (pdf 385, 398, 399, 379). Saturn is the principle of "Know thyself,
accept thyself, be thyself" — Time, through which "we came to Earth … through
Time (Saturn) and Space (Jupiter)." His gifts are "patience and introspection";
as "the Cross of Matter" attachment to mundane things "interferes with true
happiness," yet he "simplifies our lives by dissolving things we hang onto that
don't really make us happy," for "Saturn is the greatest of teachers for those
of us who are observant." (Runner-up for copy: the Stoics "cast a Saturnine pall
over philosophy and astrology alike," pdf 385.)

**Uranus** (pdf 241, 453, 459, 460, 270). "Uranus is a planet of quick insight,"
anxious "to grow, to live in the now, while Saturn seeks to protect past
traditions"; it "needs to be honored through discovery of new concepts, ideas,
friends, organizations." On transits "more than any other transiting planet, [it
forces] us to hear the Muezzin's call from the tower, to wake up," offering
"productive time out from mundane, routinized living, to detach, gain
perspective." It is "the esoteric ruler of Libra" and "the higher octave planet
for Mercury," granting "objectivity about life's situations and injustices."

**Neptune** (pdf 159, 162, 543, 546). Poseidon/Neptune is "a great dissolver or
destroyer" who used "a trident to bring down attachment" — the Lord of the Sea
who meets the Mother of Form. "Neptune is the planet of faith," required "to let
go of the past and open up to the future"; to those who dare, "Neptune is a
liberator," for "Poseidon does not dissolve anything we still need." He is "not
about apathy … but about hearing and acting upon what God tells us to do in the
outer world"; his message is "Relax. Let go and let God have some room."

**Pluto** (pdf 295, 40, 41, 556). "Pluto, appropriately, is Hades, Lord of the
Greek Underworld"; he "rules the unconscious depths of the Scorpio or Eighth
House personality." A "reclusive planet" that aids sublimation of sexual energy
into discovery, he is "the higher octave planet for Mars." Though "you may view
Pluto as a malefic planet, its prime objectives are transformation and
transcendence": he "should put you into contact with your inner resources in
time of crisis, help you face your worst fears and transcend."

**Earth** (pdf 370). Earth is not empty after all — Burt follows the esoteric
tradition that makes **Earth the esoteric ruler of Sagittarius**. In Pythagorean
key: "The Four is a solid cube … the first solid figure. It meant Earth or
matter"; "Matter is solid ground—the foundation, cornerstone, or Nadir. Earth is
the esoteric ruler of Sagittarius"; "Earth also relates to the body"; and the
ruler's teaching is balanced living, "keeping one's head in the clouds and one's
feet on the ground." Thin, but real and definitional.

**Signs** (secondary; 1–3 quotes each). All twelve signs carry at least one
chapter-opening definitional quote in `burt.json` under `about: "sign:<Sign>"` —
e.g. Aries as "the Age of the Ram, of Mars and of the Hero" (pdf 23); Taurus
earth "fertile and fecund sexuality" (pdf 59); Cancer "the Cosmic Womb" (pdf
135); Leo's "self-control—self-mastery" (pdf 183); Virgo's withdrawn, quiet
earth-mood (pdf 211); Libra the third Cardinal sign (pdf 253); Scorpio's
"turbulent swampy waters of the underworld" (pdf 295); Sagittarius' "Spirit,
expansion of awareness" (pdf 339); Capricorn's "shoulds and oughts" (pdf 379);
Aquarius "powerful, magnetic, opinionated" (pdf 447); Pisces "the cosmic ocean
of infinity" (pdf 509); Gemini "cheerful, playful, and somewhat glib" (pdf 95).

## Bodies with no usable material

None. All eleven bodies have planet-level quotes. Earth is the thinnest (3
planet quotes + 1 ruler quote, all from one page), as expected for a body
classical astrology does not rule with; the rest have 4–6 planet quotes each
plus ruler-of context.

## Dropped quotes

None dropped for illegibility. The scan is a clean 2023 Internet Archive
digitization; every quoted page has a legible text layer under the image. Two
near-misses were left out on principle, not legibility: "The Sun is the only I
AM planet; Aries is the I AM sign" spans the pdf 24→25 page break (each half is
represented instead), and mid-sentence fragments were never quoted with invented
endings. Burt's printed lowercase "sun" on pdf 178 is preserved verbatim.

## Counts and verification

- **96 entries** in `curation/sky/burt.json`: 61 `about: "planet"` (all eleven
  bodies; Earth has 3), 16 `ruler-of:*`, 19 `sign:*` — all twelve signs covered.
- **50 distinct PDF pages** cited; every one was read as a rendered page image.
- Every entry was then re-checked character-for-character against its page's
  OCR text layer as an independent second witness: all but three matched
  exactly after whitespace/quote normalization; the 3 residual differences were
  OCR spacing artifacts on the OCR side ("wasa", "rememberposeidon",
  "forexample"), adjudicated against the rendered page, which reads as
  transcribed.
- Curly quotation marks and em-dashes are normalized to ASCII in the JSON;
  spelling, capitalization (including Burt's lowercase "sun" on pdf 178) and
  word order are verbatim.
- No files outside `curation/sky/` were touched; nothing was committed.
