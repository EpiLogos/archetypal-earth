## Learned User Preferences

- Keep ProjectCentral and project docs inside `Archetypal-Earth/ProjectCentral/`; never create a loose `~/Central/Work/ProjectCentral/`.
- Merge finished feature work to `main` before deploying; production builds `main`, not a feature branch.
- Do not remove established behaviours when fixing adjacent UI (personal chart, descend-to-archetype, sky orbits, modal close staying in-mode).
- Mode chrome: closing a reading/modal stays in that mode; the no-modal state keeps options; put options in one bottom-panel control under the mode selector, matching the rest of the app rather than ad-hoc lists, toasts, or dividers.
- README contract: `README.md` is the product's front door for a GitHub visitor, in the owner's first-person voice (the landing line, `labels.landing` in `curation/provider.json`, is its opening). It links https://jung.epi-logos.org, carries one screenshot of the Self state (`docs/img/self.jpg`), and says what the field holds and how it is read, in the app's own words (the lens blurbs in `src/shell/lens.ts`). It never explains what the app already says in itself, is never the vault's README (no Obsidian/Dataview/"open the folder"), and is never a build report, ledger, pipeline note or spec. Pipeline, deploy and working notes go in `ProjectCentral/docs/`. Only the field counts and lens names are kept in step, and `tests/readme.test.ts` checks them: when the data or lenses change, run it and fix the figures, do not rewrite the README. Retake the screenshot only when the Self state looks materially different.
- Prefer unique Commons image plates per family/occurrence within the 320MB budget; slug or metadata coverage is not the same as distinct plates on the site.
- A text is a lens (owner, 30 Oct 2026; the earlier "no per-book modes" line is withdrawn): one Texts lens whose subject is a Work, entered from wherever a work is named; Aion and the Red Book stay the lenses of CW9ii and Liber Novus (`Work.lens`). The Source filter slices. A Work, a Person and the visitor (You) are nodes of the field and the graph, their ties derived from the cites, authorship, `subject` and the visitor's own engagements, never typed in.
- The visitor node is derived in the browser from the practice store and its engagement log; nothing personal reaches the URL, a share link, the field data, a build or the network.
- The Book of Symbols is quoted like any book: verbatim passages with the printed-page cite, at the length a reading needs (it is an in-print book, so short quotations, not whole pages), no paraphrase kept beside them; its plates are not taken. Every quotation is drawn by the one Quote component (`src/ui/quote.ts`).
- Field: clicking a node opens that instance (parent is already linked); default focus is The Self.
- Graph mode: the bar for interaction, feel and performance is Obsidian's graph; keep the colour-based system. Highlighted relations rise quietly (a slow breath, a soft travelling light) rather than as bright strong lines; theoretics stay small but are real bodies with physics; the focus and reframe around clicked/hovered nodes and opening readings must read as one consistent motion.
- Card and sephira readings (owner, 3 Nov 2026: what was there was "literally non-informative"): a Thoth card's page opens with a plain, direct reading of the card (its `line`, faithful to Crowley), then Crowley in whole sentences on what it means, its picture and its number or attribution — never a clause cut mid-sentence; the reading at the planet adds Burt on its sign (the sign's own page, strongest passages first) and on its planets. A sephira's place is the planet Crowley's list gives its number (Geburah at Mars, Malkuth at the Earth held as a planet): it is read there, never on the globe (`skyHome`, `#/sky/<body>/sephira/<id>`). One deck, one rule (owner, 4 Nov 2026: "theres one deck, it has a set number of cards"): all 78 cards stand on a planet's rings and are read there — a sign's Atu and the court card whose span lies mostly in the sign at the sign's ruler as Burt names it (sign ↔ planet `burt` joins), the Princesses at the Earth with the four elements, the Aces at Pluto; an Ace's or Princess's quarter of the Earth is a place it is named, not its home (`scripts/lib/sky-address.mjs`).
- Astrology/sky: orbits and options belong in default sky mode, not walk-gated; modal X closes to sky with orbits (do not force the birth-chart view); default view is the zoomed-out sky with the chart (not walk-gated); drop snapshot/radial-scale text dumps; prefer a near-live ephemeris-driven sky; orbit-node click stays at the planet, gazes at Earth (floats up) and float click descends; descend looks at Earth, then drops, then settles—open the reading only after descend finishes; return to sky zooms out a little, orients to the planet, then zooms to it; planet pick in-sky approaches without a forced zoom-out; subjects with sky ties keep a persistent “See in sky” (not ad-hoc “back to sky”).

## Learned Workspace Facts

- Archetypal Earth is `~/Central/Work/Archetypal-Earth` (GitHub remote `EpiLogos/archetypal-earth`), with its own docs and `ProjectCentral`.
- Cite and ingest use the vault at `~/Documents/books/jung-archetypal-field` (`VAULT=...` for theory, astrology, practice, depth, aionbook, dynamics-route, and images work).
- After new plates land in `public/data/images.json`, run ingest so `field.json` carries them (`VAULT=$HOME/Documents/books/jung-archetypal-field npm run ingest`), then `npm run images:verify`.
- The dynamical lens is a route: `curation/dynamics-route.json` → `scripts/dynamics-route.mjs` → `public/data/dynamics-route.json` / `src/dynamics/figures.ts`; the e2e gate is `tests/ui/e2e/dynamics-route.mjs`.
- In `scripts/lib/cite.mjs`, a ¶ is cited only when the next marker is the next number; otherwise cites stay page-only.
- Dynamics/theory quotation enrichment draws from vault works `van-eenwyk` and `cw08` through the existing citation rails; quotes must be verbatim on the cited pages.
- Elements/alchemy/sky/Thoth plan: `ProjectCentral/docs/ELEMENTS-ALCHEMY-SKY-TAROT-PLAN.md`. Alchemy is its own lens (not a Theory subsection). Elemental earth family id `earth-element` (display “Earth”); sky planet body key `terra`. Quintessence, ether, and spirit are distinct, not aliases. Number archetypes extend through ten with Sephiroth; the tree family is the join.
- Thoth deck work uses plates from `~/Central/Work/Quaternal-Tarot`; Book of Thoth is under `~/Documents/books`. Families cover majors, minors, suits, ranks↔1–10, and courts as archetypes.
- Place has two spheres: `lat/lon/geoPrecision` is the Earth, `Occurrence.sky` the sky (a body, a ring of it, an ecliptic span), independent of each other. One derivation, `scripts/lib/sky-address.mjs`, feeds the card rings (`sky-cards.mjs`) and the occurrences (`ingest.mjs`); `tests/data/sky-address.test.ts` holds that they agree. Plan and acceptance: `ProjectCentral/docs/PLACE-AND-ATTESTATION-PLAN.md`.
- The curated families (signs, decans, sephiroth, elements, functions, the Thoth deck) are attested by occurrences built from their pages, one per family per work (`scripts/lib/attest.mjs`, dated by `curation/works.json` or the field's own cites). A family with no member fails `tests/data/field.test.ts`; "named in the field, no instance yet" is never a one-line, and the focus label never claims an absence.
- A family's members are its own occurrences, its co-manifests and the instances its vault note lists (`Occurrence.coFamilyIds` is the rule; the client's `famMem` / `archMem` / `theoMem` read it for threads, labels, counts and focus, while `famOcc` stays each family's home list so the Aion, Red Book and scope modes are not widened). A merge key in `curation/families.json` is an alias, never a node.
