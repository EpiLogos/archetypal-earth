## Positions

The site counts its positions **from zero** and states them in several registers. The operator geometry (§ I, "The
Self and the 0/1 Operator"):

> "#0 (The Origin / 0) — The indeterminate ground before anything is spoken. Pure capacity.
> #1 (State 00) — The indeterminate relating to the indeterminate. Pure potentiality, the psychoid shadow where forms
> remain asleep.
> #2 (State 01) — The indeterminate relating to the determinate. The emergence of form, the intrusion of the archetype
> into lived experience.
> #3 (State 10) — The determinate relating to the indeterminate. The ego reaching back toward the unconscious, the act
> of symbol-making.
> #4 (State 11) — The determinate relating to the determinate. Conscious rationality, the explicit mapping of known
> terms.
> #5 (The Synthesis / 1) — The final return where the structure recognises itself. The quintessence that gathers the
> fourfold back into unity."

With its own gloss: "This is why the quaternity is canonical. A structure generated by a 0/1 operator invariably
yields this exact geometry: the two vertical poles of origin and synthesis holding four explicate interaction
states." And: "The positions (0 through 5) are not empty slots; they determine themselves through six successive
layers of meaning." (§ VI)

The six layers (§ VI, "The six layers of self-determination"), as the site's table gives them:

| Layer | Question | What it provides |
| --- | --- | --- |
| #0 Gebser structures | Why? | The consciousness-stadial ground (Archaic to Supermental) |
| #1 Name (P-series) | What? | The ontological articulation (Truth to Image) |

The P-series (§ VI, Layer #1) runs **#0 Truth (Anuttara), #1 Mind (Svātantrya), #2 Word (Vāk), #3 Logos (Mahāmāyā),
#4 Son / Jīva (Nara), #5 Image / Jāgrat (Pratyabhijñā)**. The conjugate P'-series (§ VI, Layer #2) runs **#0' Play
(Līlā), #1' Need, #2' Sacrifice, #3' Decision, #4' Love, #5' Work (Gocarī)**, and: "The entire P and P' series

Layer #3 holds twelve MEF lenses, each breaking down internally "into its own 6-fold (.0 through .5)": Day —

## Notation

As used on the site:

- Positions are written `#0` … `#5`; the conjugate series takes a prime: `#0'` … `#5'` ("the P and P' series").
- The return is written `5→0` (prose renders the literal `5\to0`), and `5→0 (Return)` in the dream count.
- Recursion is dot notation with ranges, not a dash: "its own 6-fold (.0 through .5)", CF5 "(4.0/1–4.4/5)", CF6
  "(4.5/0)"; Context Frames are written `(00/00)`, `(0/1)`, `(0/1/2)`, `(0/1/2/3)`, `(5/0)`.
- Arrows `→` connect phases and arcs (`#0 → corners`, `0/(0/1)→0/1`); diagonal arrows `↘ ↗` appear only as figure
  labels (`#5 ↘ 4 bottom orbs`, `#5' ↗ 4 top orbs`).
- Bracket syntax carries meaning (§ IV): "The parentheses () and square brackets [] act as structural containers,
  expressing the dynamics of transcendence and immanence."

Not on the site, from the notes only: the recursion dash (`#2-0`, `#2-5`, S1) and `6 ≡ 0 mod 6` (S4). No `#n-m` and
no `mod 6` appears anywhere on the page or in its bundle; the site's count returns through `5→0`.

## On number, Jung, the psychoid, time

Number (W, § II): "Pauli realised that whole numbers behave with the exact same double-character as Jung's
archetypes. As quantity, they stand outside us, countable and exact. As image and ordering power, they stand inside
us, amplifying themselves through mythological and symbolic statements. Pauli concluded that natural numbers are the
most primitive archetypes—inside and outside at once, devised by mind and discovered as order." And Pauli: "I cannot
acknowledge an antithesis between a mathematical and a symbolic description of nature, since for me the mathematical
representation is a symbolic description par excellence."

The archetypal numbers one to six appear as lens L0' (W, § VI): "The psychoid numbers themselves as archetypes. One
(unity, the monad), Two (polarity, reflection), Three (triad, synthesis), Four (quaternity, stability), Five (pentad,
quintessence), Six (hexad, perfect completion)." No sentence on the site states a rule mapping number *n* to position
*#n* or *#(n−1)*; there is no `#6` anywhere — the count returns at `5→0`.

The generative count is the Egg Dream (W, § II): "0/1 (The Frame)" → "1 (The Seed) — … The first unity" → "2
(Polarity)" → "3 (The Third)" → "4 (Quaternity)" → "5 (Quintessence)" → "5→0 (Return)" → "0 (Archetypal Image)":
"In QL terms, the visible seven are the unfolding; the hidden eighth is the field of manifestation—the 0/1 condition
that allows the dream to appear."

The psychoid (W): "The archetype, as Jung finally understood it, is psychoid: it is as much inside as outside, an
ordering pattern that manifests simultaneously in the soul's images and the world's matter." (§ I) · "If the 4+2
geometry is not merely a psychological projection but a psychoid fact—an ordering pattern that structures both the
soul and the physical world—then it must appear in the exact sciences." (§ II) · "The underlying QL positions—the six
pure psychoid numbers, the P/P' invariants—do not change." (§ VI)

Jung's functions (W, § VI, lens L1'): "The four psychic functions—Sensation, Feeling, Thinking, Intuition—bounded by
the poles of Introversion and Extroversion." S1's six-position mapping ("#0: Pre-conscious/unconscious ground" …
"#5: Transcendent function (synthesis)") is not on the site; the site's is a 4+2, not a six.

Time: the site says nothing about synchronicity — no occurrence of "synchron-" anywhere. Time appears only as the
medium of the structure's traversal ("ratio, when given time and vibration, becomes music", § V; the ten phases of
the Diamond figure, each with a musical-ratio duration) and as Gebser's registers ("pre-temporal" Archaic, "time-free"
Integral, § VI). Von Franz appears nowhere on the site.

## How the lens uses it

The brief sets the mapping: archetypal numbers 1–6 ↔ QL positions #0–#5. So one stands at #0, two at #1, three at #2,
four at #3, five at #4, and six at #5. In the Theory lens each number shows von Franz's and Jung's words (verbatim,
page-cited from the corpus) beside the QL position's own formulation (quoted from S1, marked QL). The lens never
presents a QL formulation as Jung's or von Franz's, nor the reverse.

## Gaps and tensions

- **Two alignments of number to position — ruled.** The brief and S4 align from **one** (1 at #0, six at #5). S2's table
  aligns von Franz's sequence from **zero**: "0: Void-plenitude, undifferentiated potential", "1: Unity", …, "6:
  Perfect completion via circular return". The site counts positions 0–5, has no #6, and states no mapping rule; its
  Egg Dream count puts "1 (The Seed)" after the 0/1 frame, and its L0' coincidences ("2 (Polarity)" ↔ "Two (polarity,
  reflection)", "4 (Quaternity)" ↔ "Four (quaternity, stability)", "5 (Quintessence)" ↔ "Five (pentad, quintessence)")
  are displayed, never stated as a rule. Asked on 2026-10-10, the owner ruled the brief's alignment canonical: the
  lens keeps **1–6 ↔ #0–#5**; S2's zero-based table stands as the recorded alternative.
- **Five and six in von Franz.** Number and Time says much less about five and six than about one to four. The lens
  shows what is there and says so.
- **Provenance mix in the lens.** The QL formulations the lens shows beside each number are still S1's; the site does
  not carry per-position formulations in that register, so nothing on the site contradicts them directly — but they
  are the notes' words, not the published page's.
