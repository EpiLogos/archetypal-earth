## How the lens uses it

The brief sets the mapping: archetypal numbers 1–6 ↔ QL positions #0–#5. So one stands at #0, two at #1, three at #2,
four at #3, five at #4, and six at #5, where "6 ≡ 0 mod 6" (S4) closes the circle back to #0. In the Theory lens each
number shows von Franz's and Jung's words (verbatim, page-cited from the corpus) beside the QL position's own
formulation (quoted from S1, marked QL). The lens never presents a QL formulation as Jung's or von Franz's, nor the
reverse.
