# Granular audit — mode drift against the founding commissions (9 October 2026)

Four passes, all evidence-backed: a code-level card audit of every mode against the core
anatomy (`src/ui/deep.ts` / `src/ui/reveal.ts`), two functional browser reproductions
(headless Chromium, state dumps via `window.__earth`, 87 screenshots preserved under
`.cache/screens/audit-2026-10-09/`), and a temporal/dynamical dossier. Measured against the
founding texts themselves: `docs/SPEC.md` (§4C time, §6 image-first, §9 interaction quality,
§13 relations, §14 never-list, §17 states), `docs/SKY-SPEC.md` §1, and the Van Eenwyk lane's
own harvest (`~/Documents/books/jung-archetypal-field/wiki/maps/chaos-dynamics.md`).

Verdict up front: the owner's complaints all reproduce. None of them is a taste dispute.
Each names a commission that exists on disk and is not being met.

## 1. SKY — the links work; the camera is hijacked; the closeup was never built

**S1 (HIGH, the "links are not working" complaint) — every sky→earth descent lands in the
same wrong pose.** All 35 ties resolve; every tie button navigates to the correct field
node. But in 14/14 tested clicks (Mercury, Saturn, Sun cards; card-footer "Descend"; the
"On the earth" occurrence rows; the birth-sky descent), the camera never travels to the
node: it lands at the sky camera's own lat/lon carried down to world distance. Proof by
contrast: `#/f/sol-niger` directly frames the node at (47.4, 8.35, 1.9); the same focus via
Saturn's tie lands at (15.7, 65.2, 3.77). For tight subjects the click reads as dead.
Root cause: on leaving the sky, `syncSky`'s descent flight (`src/app/controller.ts:730-739`)
starts *after* `enterFocus`/`enterManifest` have already started their framing flight, and
`CameraRig.flyTo` keeps only the last flight (`controls.ts:115-137`). One fix point: when
the leave-sky names a target, do not overwrite the framing flight.

**S2 (HIGH, "can't zoom down to any other planet") — no per-planet approach exists, and the
drawing forbids one.** Planet discs draw at a constant 8–15 px regardless of distance
(`src/sky/layer.ts:404`, `bodyPx` by log10 radius); they fade out below 400 R⊕ and go
unpickable below ~894; approaching Mars' disc ends at a black Earth night-side. The only
camera flights are to the Earth or the system home — `SkyLayer.positionOf` (layer.ts:486-489)
is dead code. The stage model has no body stage. Fix: a per-planet approach stage (look-at
eases to the planet's drawn position; the Moon's distance-scaled disc treatment at
layer.ts:416-419 is the in-repo precedent; pick/fade gates need per-body exceptions near an
open card).

**S3 (MED, "zoom logic is weird") — mechanics are sound; composition is not.** Continuous
wheel out-and-back is reversible to 0.00° with no stage seizures. What feels wrong: (a) mid-
handoff (600–1500 R⊕) *nothing is centred* — Earth exits top-right as the Sun arrives
bottom-left; (b) gesture-entry reaches the system at an arbitrary tilt while the S-key path
composes a canonical view — two doors into the same room; (c) a 2.2× dead zone past system
home; (d) the first pull-back can stall on the pre-sky-load maxDist clamp. Fix: keep a
subject centred through the handoff; settle gesture arrivals toward the canonical orientation
after the wheel rests; trim the dead zone.

## 2. WALK & BASE STATES — a dead launch button, a trap state, both-modes-at-once

**W1 (HIGH, "can't double-click a card to focus it in reading mode") — the binding does not
exist.** `grep dblclick src/` finds two hits, both in the graph's zoom filter. No card
surface — reveal card, strip caption, thumbnails, Aion stop, Red Book stop — has a
double-click handler. Measured per surface with screenshots. Worse: strip thumbnails
double-click to the WRONG STEP (the rail re-centres between taps, i=5 → i=9,
`src/ui/strip.ts:83-90`). Fix: dblclick → `setDeep(true)` on card surfaces (controller has
it at controller.ts:357-360); debounce/freeze `center()` under thumbnail double-click.

**W2 (HIGH, "Red Book doesn't launch properly") — the launch is dead, and the null state is
a trap.** `toggleRedbook` navigates to `{redbook:{}}`, but `stateEq`
(`src/state/store.ts:158-166`) compares only `redbook?.stop`/`?.genesis` — never the
presence of `redbook` — so that state is "equal" to the plain world and `navigate` refuses
it. Consequences, all reproduced: the Red Book switch does nothing from the plain world;
Esc, the card's close button, and the R toggle are ALL dead inside the null state (hash
stuck at `#/redbook`); only picking a stop un-traps, and walking back re-traps. `skyEq`
distinguishes `{}` from absent — that is why Sky launches. One-line class of fix: compare
`!!a.redbook === !!b.redbook` in `stateEq`.
The owner's design expectation stands on the field's own convention: the graph's fresh null
state already centres The Self (`prime: true`); Red Book's null renders `stops[0]` instead
(view.ts:52). Null landing = The Self is the fix.

**W3 (HIGH) — Aion renders underneath Red Book.** Entering Red Book from Aion leaves both
modes on screen — colliding headings, the zodiac clock over the folio rail
(controller.ts:509-516 returns before `prev.history` cleanup runs at :542-547).

**W4 (MED) — stale "Resume tour" transport after the walk is gone.** The play button
re-labels only when the icon state flips (`src/ui/time-control.ts:237-243`), so
`setTour(null)` while paused leaves "Resume tour" wired to the field clock.

**W5 (LOW) — `#/aion` deep-link lands on the plain globe instead of the default reading the
button picks (router.ts:95-99 vs controller.ts:865-868); Red Book never sets
`document.title`.**

## 3. CARDS — the image-first law is met only in the core

SPEC §6: "Every meaningful entity: strong representative image set, hero image…". The core
meets it structurally: `plate()` renders unconditionally with a tonal palette fallback
("never a broken-image icon") and an occurrence→family image fallback.

**C1 (HIGH) — Aion epochs can never carry an image.** `showEpoch` has no plate call; the
`Epoch` type has no image field (0/7, structural). Events render a hero only from the first
resolvable occurrence's *own* image — **3/45, 0/19, 0/22** across the three readings; the
core's fallback would give 29/45, 1/19, 13/22.

**C2 (HIGH) — Red Book has no working images in production.** 22/37 stops render no hero at
all; 15/37 render a facsimile through a dev-server-only route that 404s in every build.
The core's `plate()` + `occurrenceImage` fallback covers 37/37.

**C3 (HIGH) — Jung's words are behind collapsed disclosures with off-pattern cites.** All
147 Aion passages sit behind "Read the source"; cites render as raw keys
("cw09ii · ¶149, n.83 (pdf p105)") where the core says "Jung · Aion (CW9ii) · 1951 · ¶149".
Burt's quotes are likewise collapsed; sky `passages()` drops the year `jungLine` carries.

**C4 (HIGH) — 100% of Aion card prose is agent summary, and it narrates.** The type admits
it ("the site's summary (not the author's words)"). 27 excuse-pattern strings render to
readers, worst: "The atlas uses two equal 2,143-year months…", "not softened here", "the
vault keeps it as his claim, not history (S)", "S-correction, kept beside the claim".
yearDisplay suffixes are metadata dumps rendered verbatim ("1950 · Aion — THE forecast",
"After 216 CE · lower-bound display year").

**C5 (MED) — Red Book leaks.** Four stops render plate-inventory paragraphs
(`PLATES (p0024.jpg…`) into body text — the `PLATES (`/`PLATES:` variants defeat the
`PLATE (` filter; cite footers use raw slugs ("Jung · liber-novus"); genesis doctrine
strings embed raw CW keys; four wordy link labels vs the core's bare-name rows.

**C6 (MED) — Sky drift.** The card is now a dossier (position fifth; SPEC §10 pressure);
tie notes re-narrate what the basis chip already says; "the generated sky" jargon survives
in card text; the Phase-7 texture deferral leaves eleven image-less bodies next to an
image-led core (the deferral, not the lit sphere, is the cop-out).

## 4. TIME — one scalar spent on alpha

**T1 (HIGH)** — the entire temporal system is one presence scalar spent between two fixed
visual states (live point / hollow ghost, hard 0.2 alpha floor). No node emerges or fades
at its own date; a node's year is used exactly twice (slider position, one visibility
sample). SPEC §4C commissions "animate emergence and disappearance, see patterns
move/split/condense/migrate" — none of which exists: no per-node lifecycle, no arcs
animating along the cursor (the arc machinery is welded to tours), no era populations, no
era clustering (the graph's forces are time-blind).

**T2 (HIGH) — a real seam:** in Aion, the standing event's own occurrences sit at
`vis = 0` under the cursor — rendered as ghosts and unpickable at the moment the reading
stands on them (`timeVisibility < 0.5` gate; contradicts view.ts's own "never invisible,
never unclickable" comment).

**T3 (MED)** — temporal absence and attentional absence are visually indistinguishable
(one shader gate multiplies both gates); focus does not scope the time control to the
subject's span or build the subject's chronology (only the explicit thread walk does).

**T4 (MED)** — Red Book has no temporal conditioning at all (static per-stop emphasis;
snapshot/restore of the timeline on enter/leave); the walk never tunes the field to the
folio's moment; the stop's era is text only.

## 5. THE DYNAMICAL MODE — commissioned by omission, delivered by omission

The Van Eenwyk external-lens lane harvested the complete intellectual basis into the vault
(`wiki/maps/chaos-dynamics.md`: archetypes as attractor-generating patterns, strange
attractors "never repeating—yet always resembling", mandalas as fractal attractors
pp.110-111, enantiodromia as phase transition, health at the chaotic edge — every row
J/V-labelled with corpus cites) and staged 11 copyrighted figures in
`curation/van-eenwyk-figures/` with a licence note reserving promotion to the owner.
Its two sister lanes from the same harvest became full site modes (Aion: `76e4b52`; Red
Book: `b40cfb4`). The third lane stopped at curation: **no code, no data pipeline, no UI
surface, no spec document exists** — the figures are referenced by nothing.

What building it entails (from the dossier): the data is already in `field.json` —
`spectrum.position` as the order parameter (SPEC §16 made spectrum the governing
parameter), families as basins, parallels as correlated trajectories, 2,745 dated
occurrences as the field's history; the graph's d3 simulation is already an n-body
dynamical system whose live iteration nothing observes. The licence-gated plates cannot
ship; natively rendered Lorenz/Mandelbrot equivalents are original work and need no
licence. The V-labelling law holds: Van Eenwyk's dynamical vocabulary never migrates into
Jung's voice.
